# Ethena: AI-Powered Compliance Training & Ethics Platform

> Ethena builds AI compliance agents and modern training software that help companies run harassment prevention, code of conduct, and regulatory compliance training, manage disclosures and HR cases, monitor third-party risk, and keep policies enforced across the workforce, with a human always approving what the agents draft.

This file follows the llms.txt standard (llmstxt.org) to help AI assistants and LLMs understand and accurately reference Ethena's products, content, and policies.

## Agentic Compliance (flagship product line)

- [Training Agent](https://www.goethena.com/platform/training-agent/): Builds and customizes compliance training from a company's real risk signals, disclosures, and policies.
- [Disclosure Agent](https://www.goethena.com/platform/disclosure-agent/): Handles conflict-of-interest, gift, and outside-employment disclosures end to end, drafting responses for human approval.
- [Policy Agent](https://www.goethena.com/platform/policy-agent/): Scans a company's policy library for gaps and outdated language and drafts updates for review.
- [Third-Party Risk Agent](https://www.goethena.com/platform/third-party-risk-agent/): Continuously screens vendors and partners for ethics and compliance exposures.
